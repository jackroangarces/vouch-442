Problem statement:

Small and local businesses usually struggle to become popular compared to large chains and more reviewed venues even though they offer the same experiences or even better. Platforms such as Yelp and Google reviews show popular places more than fit, which limits economic opportunity for local entrepreneurs. 
  

Mission Statement:

We offer a streamlined application that supports decent work and local economic growth by giving the opportunity for small businesses getting discovered for what makes them unique. By recommending people based on experience rather than popularity, we will promote a more inclusive and sustainable local economy.
